    The power of choice — banker vs broker.
    Transition Lending

    The power of choice.

    You're a Modern Mortgage Banker — with the control of a bank and the pricing flexibility of a broker.

    Banker vs broker

    Two channels · One LO

    Use both channels to serve every client. Each comes with its own advantages — you choose which fits each deal.

    Banking matters

    In-house control · faster closings

    • Early CD issuance — we control the timeline
    • Docs early when needed
    • Fund early when needed
    • Use in-house AMCs for faster appraisals
    • Eat lock extensions easily when needed
    • Issue lender credit with ease
    • No back-end compensation disclosure
    • Double app without disclosing on challenging files
    • More control over the client experience

    Brokering matters

    Pricing · product · client-first

    • Pricing, pricing, pricing — always competitive
    • Product, product, product — never limited
    • Never worry about competitor offerings
    • Get the best deal for the client — comp stays the same
    • Better rates through 90+ wholesale channels
    • No commission caps — production = income
    • Full database ownership
    • 1099 options (based on state)
    • Less overhead & more transparency

    You have the power of choice.

    Every loan, every deal — you decide which channel serves your client best.

    👤
    LO Workflow

    Loan officer — stage by stage

    StageActions
    Lead / app management
    First point of contact with borrower
    Send ARIVE apply link
    Inform borrower to submit docs in ARIVE
    Update LOA with pre-approval info
    Pull credit (LOA can pull if licensed)
    Communicate file ready for pre-approval
    Pre-approval received
    Pre-approval details received from LOA
    Connect with borrower on options/scenarios/rate
    Update LOA with any structure changes
    Issue pre-approval letter to borrower
    Contract readiness
    Send contract & escrow info to LOA
    Send loan amount, rate & investor info
    Lock loan once LOA confirms all docs received
    Keep borrower & agents informed on milestones
    Submission to processing/UW
    Maintain closing coordination with processor
    Work closely with LOA/processor on concerns
    Follow up with processor through funding
    Coordinate signing & timely closure
    🤝
    LOA Workflow

    Loan officer assistant — stage by stage

    StageActions
    New lead / app submitted
    Review app for completeness
    Flag missing or inconsistent info
    Confirm employment type: W-2, 1099, self-employed
    Send VOE & calculate income
    Collect & organize docs through ARIVE
    Send welcome email & needs list
    Pre-approval prep
    Pull property tax & calculate taxes
    Look up HOA/solar in Zillow/Redfin
    Verify income per VOE
    Pull product & pricing per LO notes
    Run AUS (DU or LP)
    If ready → LO issues pre-approval letter
    Offer accepted / contract
    LOA orders fees & title documents
    Update ARIVE with contract & property info
    Upload FNMA 3.4 to investor portal
    Confirm product, pricing, locked or floating with LO
    Submission to processing/UW
    Prepare UW cover letter
    Confirm ARIVE organized & docs labeled
    Submit loan to processing in ARIVE
    Email processor: fee sheet, lock confirmation, cover letter
    Follow up until disclosures signed
    ⚡
    Processor Workflow

    Processor — full pipeline flow

    Phase 1 · DisclosuresPhase 2 · Title & appraisalPhase 3 · UnderwritingPhase 4 · Clear to close
    File assigned — loan set up in ARIVE
    File reviewed: income, assets, credit, LOS match
    Loan disclosures prepared
    Disclosure draft sent to LO for approval
    Initial disclosures sent for signature
    Escrow title items requested
    Title items reviewed — corrections if needed
    Confirm disclosures signed
    Order appraisal once ITP received
    AUS re-ran before submission
    Submit loan to lender for UW
    Order credit supplements as needed
    Download & communicate conditions
    Work conditions with third parties
    Prepare & send CD once LO approves
    Submit clear to close with lender
    Request final fees from escrow
    Loan docs prepared upon CTC
    Communicate with LOA/LO & escrow through funding
    Ensure docs labeled & FSS received
